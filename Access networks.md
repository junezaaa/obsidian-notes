Q: How to connect end systems to edge router?
- residential access nets
- institutional access network (KUWIN, company internet)
- mobile access networks (Wifi, 4G/5G)

#### Cable-based access
	relies on hybrid fiber coax (HFC)
- HFC Transmission rate: 
	- Downstream (Download) : 40 Mbps - 1.2 Gbps 
	- Upstream (Upload): 30-100 Mbps 
- network of cable, fiber attaches homes to ISP router
	-  homes share access network to cable headed

#### Digital subscriber line
- use existing telephone line to central office DSLAM
	- data over DSL phone line goes to Internet
	- voice over DSL phone line goes to telephone net
- DSL Transmission rate: 
	- Downstream (Download) : 24–52 Mbps 
	- Upstream (Upload): 3.5-16 Mbps

#### Wireless access networks
	shared wireless access network connects end system to router
- via base station aka "access point"
- Wireless local area networks
	- typically within or around building (100 ft)
	- **802.11b/g/n** (**generations of Wi-Fi technology**) (Wifi): 11,54,450 Mbps trans rate
#### Cellular access networks
- provided 
- by mobile, cellular network operator (10's km)
- 10s Mbps	- 4G/5G cellular networks 
####  Enterprise networks
- companies, uni , etc.
- mixed of wired, wireless tech, switch and router 
#### Data center networks
- high-bandwidth links (10s to 100s Gbps) connect 1000s of server together, and to Internet
